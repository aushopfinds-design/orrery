# THE FATE KEY — Career & Money Reading research and implementation notes

Research checked 28 October 2026 for **Success, Money & Your Future**. The references below were used to compare public report structures and traditional interpretation topics. Their prose, illustrations and distinctive visual design were not copied.

## Korean service and learning references

1. **SajuSpot — Wealth reading**: https://www.sajuspot.kr/saju/money.php
   - The public product page presents separate questions for money structure, income growth, spending/saving and business/investment, and describes a paid personalized reading based on birth details.
   - Applied here: answer earning style, evidence, work/business implications and timing as distinct customer questions.
2. **60Saju — Wealth report**: https://60saju.com/wealth
   - The sample page advertises a Saju chart, money-pattern card, five-element visualization, ten-year timeline and annual cards.
   - Applied here: include a Four Pillars table, visible five-element counts, cycle timeline and ten-year table. Its sample gauges and investment prescriptions were not reused; this report shows no invented 0–10 fortune scale or investment directive.
3. **SajuPrime — Practical Saju knowledge**: https://sajuprime.co.kr/knowledge
   - Its public topic index groups career aptitude with Five Elements/Ten Gods, wealth with Direct/Indirect Wealth and output-to-wealth relationships, and business timing with strength and decade/year cycles.
   - Applied here: display regular-money/opportunity-money, output, responsibility, resource and peer relationships alongside the chart’s structure and timing. Presence alone is not treated as wealth or a business verdict.
4. **KFT Korean Fortune Teller — Saju learning guide**: https://koreansaju.com/learn/
   - Its guide explains the reading sequence as chart structure, Five Elements and Ten Gods, then strength/useful-element discussion and Daewoon; it also describes the month pillar’s solar-term basis.
   - Applied here: connect annual references with the active decade cycle and preserve visible, hidden and seasonal evidence separately. A unique Yongshin is not asserted when the current conservative rule withholds one.

## Product decisions

- The report keeps seven customer-facing chapters, led by the questions in MASTER DIRECTIVE 02.
- The first chapter now places the actual Four Pillars and Five Elements chart beside the direct summary.
- The money chapter separates visible/principal markers from hidden stems, then visualizes only structure routes recognized by the existing bounded structural resolver.
- The employment/business comparison reports the actual roles present in the chart and does not force a winner.
- The turning-point chapter displays calculated Daewoon start dates. The next-ten-years chapter checks each annual pillar against the natal chart and the active Daewoon, with calculation evidence available for every row.
- The chart layer preserves the existing `FATE-qualitative-r1` and `FATE-ordinary-structure-r1` limits. Mixed, unknown-time and exceptional cases remain unresolved where required; no luck score, salary, profit or event promise is invented.
- The production Stripe price was checked read-only: Success, Money & Your Future is currently **US$10.99**. No price or payment setting was changed.

## Limits still requiring expert review

- The comparison is research into public product pages and learning material, not a certification of a single Saju school or a substitute for a qualified practitioner’s review.
- Existing conservative strength and structure policies intentionally withhold a universal strength or Yongshin verdict in ambiguous charts.
- Ten calendar-year labels use the existing mid-July annual reference point; Saju year boundaries still follow the solar-term convention and are explained in the report.
- Independent almanac verification and fluent English editorial review remain separate release gates; automated variety tests do not prove expert correctness or customer satisfaction.
